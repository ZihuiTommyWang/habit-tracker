# Weekly Habits

A simple weekly habit accountability tracker. A fixed list of habits with weekly count goals ("Gym at least 3×/week", "Unhealthy snacks at most 3×/week") — tap **+** each time you do one, and the counts reset every Monday while past weeks are kept in History with per-habit streaks.

**Live app:** https://zihuitommywang.github.io/habit-tracker/

## Install on your phone

1. Open the link above in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**
3. It opens full-screen with its own icon, like a normal app — and works offline

## Notes

- No accounts, no server. Your data is saved in the browser on the device you use it on.
- Use **Edit → Export backup** to save your data as JSON (for records, or to move it to another device via **Import backup**).
- Habits are editable in the **Edit** tab: rename, change targets, switch between "at least" and "at most" goals, add or remove habits. Removing a habit keeps its past history.
